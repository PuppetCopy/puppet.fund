Puppet - Seamless copytrading

A trader (human/machine) operates a smart wallet across chains and DeFi venues: an automated agent over the API, or a human, normally through the Puppet Wallet extension, which connects to any dapp. They trade their own money, exactly as they do today, and every action the wallet takes is gated through a co-signed, Intent-based attestation. On the same trades they earn performance fees and fame, because the capital of those who follow them trades alongside their own.

Puppets (investors) fund once and copy many. A single deposit can back every trader they choose, each under rules they sign: how much can be allocated, how fast, and how often. Capital is only pulled when a trader actually trades, so one balance follows many traders at once. The same attestation enforces these rules, so a trader can only ever act within what each backer signed for.

Funds stay in smart wallets their owners control, never the protocol, and wallet actions are permissionlessly co-signed. Each backer's exposure is bounded by their signature, not by trust. Backers hold transferable shares of a trader's pool and can redeem them at the pool's attested value.
